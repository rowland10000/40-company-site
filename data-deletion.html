資料刪除說明

最後更新 2026.11.20

「40 Threads Scanner」只會保存 Threads 公開貼文的資料，最多 60 天後自動刪除。如果你希望我們提早刪除和你的 Threads 帳號有關的資料，可以照下面的方式申請。

怎麼申請

1. 寫信到 office@40-company.com，主旨寫「Threads 資料刪除申請」。

2. 信中寫上你的 Threads 帳號名稱（@ 後面那串）。為了確認是本人，請用該帳號在 Threads 私訊或留言給 @rowland10000「已寄出刪除申請」，或附上能證明帳號屬於你的說明。

3. 我們會在收到申請後 10 個工作天內刪除相關資料，並回信通知你已完成。

取消授權

如果你曾經授權「40 Threads Scanner」存取你的 Threads 帳號，可以在 Threads 的「設定 → 帳號 → 網站權限」找到它並按「移除」。移除後，這個工具就無法再透過你的授權存取任何資料，我們也會一併刪除與你授權相關的資料。

更完整的資料處理方式，請見隱私權政策。

Data Deletion Instructions (English)

40 Threads Scanner stores only public Threads post data and deletes it automatically after at most 60 days. To have data related to your Threads account deleted sooner:

1. Email office@40-company.com with the subject "Threads data deletion request" and your Threads username.

2. To confirm the account is yours, send a direct message or reply to @rowland10000 on Threads from that account saying you have sent the request, or include other proof of ownership.

3. We will delete the data within 10 business days and confirm by email.

If you previously authorized the app, you can revoke it at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Remove. We will also delete any data associated with that authorization. See our Privacy Policy for details.
